What EC Group does

EC Group stores products for brands, packs every order and delivers it to the customer, then handles the returns. Everything runs in-house from one warehouse in Essex, with delivery across the UK and worldwide.

216years in warehousing and logistics, since 1810
4 acresof warehouse space in Essex, UK

How an order moves through EC Group

The core of the business is this cycle, which runs for every order a client's customer places.

Store

Client's stock is received and held in the Essex warehouse.

Pick and pack

Orders arrive automatically from the online store and are packed.

Deliver

Parcels ship to customers in the UK and worldwide.

Returns

Returned items are received, checked and restocked.
